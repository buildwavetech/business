Pricing & Payment Policy

Transparent pricing for all our services.

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Buildwave Tech believes in transparent and fair pricing. The following Pricing & Payment Policy details our pricing structure and payment terms for our services.

1. Service Pricing Overview

All services are custom-quoted based on client requirements. Our service charges range from ₹2,000 to ₹50,000 depending on scope and complexity.

Service Starting Price
Basic Website (Landing Page) ₹2,000
Business Website (Multi-page) ₹5,000
E-Commerce Website ₹10,000
Custom Software/Web Application ₹15,000
Graphic & UI/UX Design ₹2,000
SEO & Digital Marketing (Monthly) ₹3,000
Maintenance & Support (Monthly) ₹1,000

Note: Prices are starting prices, final quote based on specific requirements.

2. Currency

  • All prices listed in Indian Rupees (INR / ₹)
  • International clients may pay in USD or other currencies at prevailing exchange rates

3. Taxes

  • Prices may be exclusive of applicable taxes
  • GST (Goods and Services Tax) will be charged as per Government of India regulations, if applicable
  • Tax invoices provided for all transactions

4. Payment Methods

  • Accepted methods: UPI, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, Net Banking, Wallets
  • Payments processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways
  • All online transactions are encrypted and secure

5. Payment Terms

  • Advance payment required before project commencement
  • For larger projects: Payment may be split into milestones (e.g., 50% advance, 50% on delivery)
  • Payment schedule communicated and agreed upon before project start
  • Invoices issued for all payments

6. Quotation Validity

  • Quotes valid for 15 days from date of issue
  • Prices may change after validity period
  • Custom quotes provided upon consultation

7. Additional Charges

  • Scope changes may incur additional charges
  • Rush/expedited delivery: May attract premium charges
  • Third-party costs (hosting, domains, premium plugins) borne by client separately
  • Revision charges may apply beyond agreed scope

8. Failed Transactions

  • If amount is debited but transaction fails, it will be auto-refunded within 5-7 business days
  • Contact contact@buildwavetech.in with transaction details for assistance
