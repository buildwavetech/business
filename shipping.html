Shipping & Delivery Policy

Information about our service delivery process.

Last updated: August 16, 2026

Buildwave Tech is a service-based company offering digital services. No physical products are shipped. All services and deliverables are provided digitally/electronically.

1. Nature of Services

  • Buildwave Tech provides digital/IT services including website development, software development, e-commerce solutions, digital marketing, graphic design, and maintenance & support.
  • All deliverables are digital in nature and delivered electronically.
  • No physical goods are shipped or delivered.

2. Service Delivery Process

  • Upon successful payment, client receives order confirmation via email.
  • Project kickoff within 24-48 hours of payment confirmation.
  • A dedicated project manager/point of contact is assigned.
  • Regular progress updates provided via email.

3. Delivery Timelines

  • Basic Website: 3-7 business days
  • Custom Website: 7-15 business days
  • Software/Web Application: 15-45 business days (depending on complexity)
  • Graphic Design: 2-5 business days
  • SEO & Digital Marketing: Ongoing service, initial setup within 3-5 business days
  • Maintenance & Support: As per service agreement
  • Note: Timelines may vary based on project complexity and client responsiveness.

4. Mode of Delivery

  • Deliverables shared via email, cloud storage (Google Drive/Dropbox), or direct deployment.
  • Website projects: Deployed directly to client's hosting server.
  • Source code: Shared via GitHub/GitLab or email as agreed.
  • Design files: Shared via email or cloud storage.
  • Login credentials: Shared securely via email.

5. Delivery Confirmation

  • Client receives a delivery confirmation email upon completion.
  • Client is required to review and acknowledge delivery within 3 business days.
  • If no response within 3 business days, delivery is considered accepted.

6. Delays

  • Company strives to meet all delivery timelines.
  • Delays may occur due to: scope changes, client's delay in providing content/feedback, technical complexities, force majeure events.
  • Client will be notified of any anticipated delays.
  • Delays caused by client do not affect payment terms.

7. Service Area

  • Services available across India and internationally.
  • No geographic restrictions on digital service delivery.
